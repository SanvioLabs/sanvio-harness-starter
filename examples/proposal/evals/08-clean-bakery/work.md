# Proposal: Online pre-orders for Juniper Bakery

## Problem

Juniper takes weekend pre-orders by phone. On Fridays the phone rings through
the morning bake, orders get written on paper, and a few go missing every week.

## Scope

A pre-order page on the existing website for Saturday and Sunday pickups.
Customers pick items and a pickup slot, pay a deposit, and get a confirmation
email. Each slot and each item stops taking orders when it reaches the limit the
bakery sets. The bakery gets one printable list per pickup slot, and a simple
staff screen where it changes items, prices, slot times and limits itself.

## Out of scope

Delivery, loyalty points, changes to the rest of the website, a mobile app,
product photography, order changes or cancellations by customers, refunds (the
bakery handles both by hand in its payment account), hosting and payment fees,
and anything after launch except fixing faults that stop an order from going
through during the first 30 days.

## Assumptions

Juniper sends the menu, prices, pickup slots, limits and deposit amount before
build starts, and sets its own deposit and refund rules. The bakery's payment
account takes online card payments, and the bakery pays its fees. The bakery's
website lets us add a page that takes payments; if it doesn't, we say so in the
first week and quote the difference before going further.
