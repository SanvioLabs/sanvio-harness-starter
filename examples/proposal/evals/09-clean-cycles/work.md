# Proposal: Online repair booking for Larch Cycles

## Problem

Larch Cycles books bike repairs by phone and a paper diary. Double bookings
happen most weeks, and customers can't see when a repair will be ready.

## Scope

A booking page on the existing website: customers choose a repair type and a
drop-off day the shop is open, and get a confirmation email that gives the
ready-by day the shop sets for that repair type. A day stops taking bookings
when it reaches the shop's daily limit. The shop gets one printable list per
day, and a simple staff screen where it changes repair types, ready-by days,
opening days and the daily limit itself, and adds, moves or cancels a booking,
so bookings taken by phone count against the daily limit too.

## Out of scope

Payments, parts ordering, a customer account area, changes to the rest of the
website, customers moving or cancelling a booking online, updates when a repair runs late, hosting and email
fees, and anything after launch except fixing faults that stop a booking from
going through during the first 30 days.

difference before going further.
