# Proposal: Online pre-orders for Juniper Bakery

## Problem

Juniper takes weekend pre-orders by phone. On Fridays the phone rings through
the morning bake, orders get written on paper, and a few go missing every week.

## Scope

A pre-order page on the existing website: customers pick items and a pickup
slot, pay a deposit, and get a confirmation email. The bakery gets one printable
list per pickup slot.

## Out of scope

Delivery, loyalty points, changes to the rest of the website, and a mobile app.

## Fees

| Role | Hours | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineer | 40 | $175 | $7,000 |

## Assumptions

Juniper provides the menu, prices and pickup slots before build starts. Payments
run through the payment account the bakery already has.
