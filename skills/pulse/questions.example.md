# Team pulse questions

Copy this file into the shared folder as questions.md, then edit it for your
team. Each question is a level-two heading holding its id (lowercase letters,
digits and hyphens), then a chip of 12 characters or fewer, the question, and
two to four options. Don't add an option labelled Other: the window adds it.

## energy
Chip: Energy
Question: How much energy did you bring to work today?
- High: I could take on something hard
- Steady: Enough for what's planned
- Low: I'm getting through the day

## focus
Chip: Focus
Question: How much of yesterday went on the work you meant to do?
- Most: Nearly all of it
- About half: Half planned, half whatever came up
- Little: Other things took the day

## blockers
Chip: Blockers
Question: What is slowing you down most right now?
- Waiting: I need something from someone else
- Unclear: I'm not sure what's wanted
- Tools: Something I use keeps getting in the way
- Nothing: Nothing is slowing me down

## meetings
Chip: Meetings
Question: How did this week's meetings feel?
- Too many: They crowded out the work
- About right: They helped and left room
- Too few: I needed more time with people

## clarity
Chip: Clarity
Question: How clear are this week's priorities to you?
- Clear: I know what matters most
- Mostly: One or two things are open
- Unclear: I'm guessing

## help
Chip: Help
Question: When did someone last help you get unstuck?
- Today: It happened today
- This week: Some time this week
- Earlier: Longer ago than that
